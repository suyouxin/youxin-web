Ascent: Otira Slide (grade 1, finish 1+)

  1. 05:29–06:07From the Otira Valley car park (896 m) up the valley track to the footbridge, about 1050 m.
  2. 06:07–07:11Up the head of the valley to the base of the Otira Slide, about 1460 m. 1 h 42 from the car park.
  3. 07:11–07:27Stop at the base of the slide.
  4. 07:27–09:07Up the slide, 1460 to 1985 m, with short stops near 1650, 1720 and 1830 m.
  5. 09:07–09:18Stop at about 1985 m, below the steep finish.
  6. 09:18–09:58Steep finish straight to the summit: 215 m of height in 40 minutes, with a pause about every 20 m of height.
  7. 09:58–10:14On Low Peak (2212 m). The track tops out about 15 m from the mapped summit.

Descent: the same way

  1. 10:14–11:05Down the steep finish, 2200 to 2015 m. 50 minutes, slower than the way up.
  2. 11:05–11:31Down to about 1860 m, then a 10-minute stop.
  3. 11:31–12:10Down the rest of the slide to its base, about 1435 m.
  4. 12:10–12:4030-minute stop at the base of the slide.
  5. 12:40–14:05Down the valley track, past the footbridge, back to the car park.

Each row is a 200 m stretch of track: the average slope of the ground along it, and the steepest single point on it. A place walked twice is listed once.

2.9 km of the track crosses terrain of 30° or more, 1.2 km of it 35° or more.

Track angle is measured along the line walked, in 50 m steps, from the watch's elevation. Terrain slope is the angle of the ground itself, from an elevation model at about 7 m per pixel, so it misses small cliffs and steps and is not an avalanche assessment.

Times are NZDT. Peak positions and heights come from OpenStreetMap; distances to them are good to a few tens of metres. High Peak (2275 m), about 430 m west of Low Peak, was not visited.
