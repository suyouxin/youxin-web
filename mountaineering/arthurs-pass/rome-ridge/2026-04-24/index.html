Ascent: lower Rome Ridge

  1. 07:50–09:20From the highway (805 m) up the Coral Track and on to Rome Ridge, to about 1510 m.
  2. 09:20–10:20Up the ridge from 1510 to 1690 m, with 11-minute stops at about 1640 and 1690 m.
  3. 10:20–11:051690 to 1837 m. The last 75 m of height took 20 minutes, over the steepest ground of the day.
  4. 11:05–11:20At the high point, 1843 m. The track leaves Rome Ridge here and turns south.

Traverse: the ridge to Avalanche Peak

  1. 11:20–13:30South along the joining ridge, 2.1 km down to its low point at 1627 m, with several stops of 5 to 10 minutes.
  2. 13:30–14:51Up the ridge to Avalanche Peak (1833 m), with a 9-minute stop at about 1795 m.
  3. 14:51–14:55On Avalanche Peak. The track passes within 6 m of the mapped summit.

Descent: Avalanche Peak Track

  1. 14:55–15:44Down to about 1550 m, then a 12-minute stop.
  2. 15:44–17:02Down the Avalanche Peak Track to about 900 m, then a 7-minute stop.
  3. 17:02–17:27Down to Arthur's Pass village (755 m). The track ends 1.3 km south of where it started.

Each row is a 200 m stretch of track: the average slope of the ground along it, and the steepest single point on it. A place walked twice is listed once, and stretches walked downhill are left out.

3.7 km of the track crosses terrain of 30° or more, 1.3 km of it 35° or more.

Terrain slope is the angle of the ground itself, read every 50 m along the track, from LINZ LiDAR elevation data averaged to about 7 m per pixel, so it misses small cliffs and steps and is not an avalanche assessment.

Times are NZST. Peak positions and heights come from OpenStreetMap. Rome Ridge continues from the high point to Mt Rolleston Low Peak (2212 m), about 900 m to the west-north-west; that part was not climbed.
