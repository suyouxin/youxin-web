Ascent: Sebastopol Ridge (II, 2)

  1. 05:46–06:30From the village up the Red Tarns track.
  2. 06:30–07:30Ascending sidle onto the ridge, reaching Sebastopol (1468 m) at about 07:30.
  3. 07:30–10:15Along the ridge crest: west with a dip to about 1350 m, then steadily up to about 1965 m.
  4. 10:15–10:40Stop at about 1965 m where the ridge steepens into the final step, about 590 m east of the summit.
  5. 10:40–11:15Diagonally left (south-west) for about 500 m across the slope above Sawyer Stream, holding 1964–1996 m.
  6. 11:15–11:46Up to the edge of the Annette Plateau at about 2205 m, then a 20-minute stop.
  7. 12:07–12:17North along the plateau edge to Mt Annette (2235 m). Track high point 2237 m.

Descent: Mueller Hut and Sealy Tarns

  1. 12:40–13:47North off the Annette Plateau to Waihī Pass (1931 m).
  2. 13:53–14:11Closest approach to Kitchener (2042 m): about 230 m away at 1967 m. Not summited.
  3. 14:17–14:40Down to about 1760–1780 m and north across Kitchener's west side.
  4. 15:30–15:52On top of Mt Ollivier (1933 m) for about 20 minutes.
  5. 16:01Over unnamed Pt 1919 on the ridge north of Ollivier.
  6. 16:13Mueller Hut, about 1817 m.
  7. 17:25Sealy Tarns, about 1250 m.
  8. 18:19Back on the valley floor near the village, 758 m.

Each row is a 200 m stretch of track: the average slope of the ground along it, and the steepest single point on it. A place walked twice is listed once.

6.2 km of the track crosses terrain of 30° or more, 2.8 km of it 35° or more.

Track angle is measured along the line walked, in 50 m steps, from the watch's elevation. Terrain slope is the angle of the ground itself, from an elevation model at about 7 m per pixel, so it misses small cliffs and steps and is not an avalanche assessment.

Times are NZDT. Peak positions and heights come from OpenStreetMap; distances to them are good to a few tens of metres. Mt Sealy and Barrow Col, south of Annette, were not visited.
